## Conclusions
1. **[INFERENCE] Yes, fundable as safety research—not as a grant-subsidized race to build a more capable decision model.** Lead with adversarially robust oversight, an explicit severe-risk pathway, and public measurement.
2. **[EXT] The old Open Philanthropy technical-safety RFP is closed; Coefficient Giving’s live founder route is Project Tailwind.** Ordinary non-PBC for-profits are not expected to receive Tailwind funding.
3. **[EXT] LTFF has closed and been replaced by EA Funds’ Transformative AI Fund (TAIF), which accepts rolling applications and typically grants $10k–$150k.**
4. **[INFERENCE] Best near-term small-team routes: TAIF, BlueDot Seed/Rapid Grants, Manifund, SFF Speculation Grants, and Foresight’s AI Nodes RFP.** Corporate and country qualifications matter below.
5. **[EXT] Earliest fixed dates: Anthropic Fellows, October 18; Foresight Nodes, October 31 at 23:59 PDT; ARIA Cybersecurity, October 31 at 14:00 GMT.** Fellows funds individuals, not companies.
6. **[EXT] UK AISI’s Alignment Project, Challenge Fund, and Systemic Safety grants are all closed.** Its Alignment Project explicitly says a similar 2026 programme is unlikely.
7. **[EXT] ARIA’s live call is narrower than “guaranteed-safe AI”: production security software, machine-checked proofs, AI-enabled verification, and usually majority-UK delivery.**
8. **[EXT] The Schmidt/DeepMind/CAIF/ARIA joint multi-agent call closed August 8 and excludes single-agent prompt-injection work and commercial product development.**
9. **[INFERENCE] A+C is the strongest immediate package; D is a credible differentiating hypothesis, not an achieved guarantee.** B needs a security-relevant specification, not merely invariance.
10. **[INFERENCE] Do not promise runway from grant submission: decisions, legal eligibility, publication permissions, and payment are separate gates.** All sources below were accessed October 14, 2026.

## 4. Reviewer preferences and avoidable rejection risks

- **[EXT] Severe risk before architecture.** Tailwind filters proposals unrelated to catastrophic AI risk. Coefficient’s [technical guide](https://coefficientgiving.org/tais-rfp-research-areas/) prioritizes control evaluations of sabotage and realistic failures; monitoring proposals need strong prompted/fine-tuned classifier baselines. **[INFERENCE]** Specify whose agent, whose trusted policy, what attacker access, and which harmful action the monitor prevents. “Better calibration” alone supplies no severe-risk theory.
- **[EXT] No universal capability prohibition.** The joint [multi-agent exclusions](https://www.cooperativeai.com/grant-guidelines/out-of-scope) reject primarily capability-improving work with safety added afterward. CAIF’s [mission](https://www.cooperativeai.com/foundation) explicitly favors differential progress over dual-use gains. Conversely, the EU frontier call deliberately funds capabilities alongside safety, and Foresight funds AI-first science. **[INFERENCE]** Disclose capability externalities; ring-fence safety milestones from JevBench competition rather than claiming all funders prohibit capable models.
- **[EXT] Formal claims must cover a meaningful deployed boundary.** ARIA rejects vague/inflated assurance, prototype-only ambition and tooling without an end-to-end security component. AI must materially accelerate verification. **[INFERENCE]** Exact label/order invariance is not a security certificate; state-norm decay is not automatically a bound on future harmful decisions.
- **[EXT] Credentials are not uniformly required.** Anthropic and Aachen explicitly prioritize execution over degrees; TAIF supports early-stage individuals. Frontier’s prior RFP accepts promising newcomers and values named expert advisers. ARIA emphasizes delivered systems, security judgment and proof engineering. **[INFERENCE]** SSDM’s negative-result ledger is useful evidence of rigor, not a substitute for a working oversight pilot.
- **[EXT] Publication requirements vary.** Foresight requires all funded code/data/outputs open-source; Aachen free-license outputs; NAIRR open/publishable results.
