# L4 public wind EPD registry screen — 2026-10-08

**Decision: no-go for admitting a 20-independent-group cohort.** This screen used official registry/PCR records only. It did not retrieve or import emissions values. The visible records establish a small candidate pool, but not 20 independent studies or a common, statistically characterized comparison set.

## Official records checked

- [International EPD System PCR 2007:08](https://www.environdec.com/pcr-library/pcr2007-08) is the registered PCR for electricity generation, including wind. Relevant current land-based examples include [Siemens Gamesa SG 3.4-132](https://www.environdec.com/library/epd1049) and [SG 5.0-145](https://www.environdec.com/library/epd22786). The former names the same Siemens Gamesa owner and IK Ingenieria practitioner as other SG declarations; turbine models are not independent inventories.
- [Vattenfall wind farms](https://www.environdec.com/library/epd1435) and [Vattenfall Nordic wind farms](https://www.environdec.com/library/epd20040) are both under PCR 2007:08 and name the same Vattenfall owner and Ramboll practitioner. The Nordic farms are a regional subset of Vattenfall's broader portfolio; the two records and their underlying farms cannot be counted as independent studies.
- [EPDItaly 013 wind-turbine PCR and linked declaration list](https://www.epditaly.it/en/pcr_/pcr-for-wind-turbines-epditaly-013/) enumerate many model-level records, but the listed owners collapse to Goldwind (including Xinjiang Goldwind), Nordex, and Envision. Multiple models, sites, and revisions from each owner do not make independent study groups. An official [Goldwind EPD](https://www.epditaly.it/en/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/4-2-Goldwind_EPD_GWH182-5.36.27.27.5V20240113.pdf) notes that differing LCA software and background LCI datasets can cause differences, so a shared PCR does not establish a shared inventory basis.

## Admission assessment

After deduplication, these records show at most a handful of owner/practitioner lineages: Siemens Gamesa, Vattenfall, Goldwind, Nordex, and Envision. This is an upper bound, not five demonstrated independent inventories. Some declarations are model variants or owner portfolio subsets; versions and updates replace or revise prior records. Turbine manufacturers and LCA practitioners recur across records, and public metadata do not establish independent background databases or independent primary inventories.

The registries identify PCRs and functional context, but the metadata do not demonstrate one common GWP characterization method across both programs, nor expose a common uncertainty interval or statistical estimate suitable for harmonized comparison. A point EPD result alone is not an uncertainty statistic. Therefore **zero records are admitted to the L4 independent-world floor from this screen**. The pool may support source-method reconnaissance, but cannot support the preregistered 20-group cohort or its uncertainty gate.

**Next action:** close this registry lead for the 20-group requirement; pursue primary inventory sources only if they identify distinct study lineages and publicly provide compatible boundary, functional unit, GWP method, and uncertainty/statistical information. Do not count model, site, regional-portfolio, or updated-version rows as independent.
