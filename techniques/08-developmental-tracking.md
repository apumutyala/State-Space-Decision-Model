# 08 — Developmental tracking during training

Written 2026-10-06. Same legend as `01`. This lab's negative results on LLC/SGLD are the frame (`reviews/03` §1.3): SGLD susceptibility estimates biased 1.3–3.3× with the bias invisible to loss-trace R-hat/ESS; per-example susceptibilities not estimable at 1.7B-LoRA; patterning lost to known-error upweighting; prospective reweighting moved loss but not decisions; standing policy "SLT/SGLD telemetry: log and study; never gate on it" (`bounded-intelligence/docs/BOUNDARIES.md` row 14). Nothing below overturns that policy; several 2025–26 papers sharpen *how* to log.

**Headline:** (1) Checkpoint-dense public suites (Pythia, PolyPythias ×50 seeds, OLMo-2) show training dynamics are consistent across seeds with identifiable outliers — a card tracked on 2–3 seeds is meaningful, one seed is not (and the lab already requires seed-averaged ground truth). (2) Probe accuracy is non-monotone across training; always report control-task selectivity. (3) Downstream *loss* is a smooth function of training loss (loss-to-loss, 2411.12925) while thresholded metrics look emergent (mirage, 2304.15004) — track per-task NLL of the decision head, not accuracy, for phase detection. (4) Mechanistic precursors can forecast capability onset per seed with calibrated intervals and a pre-registered gate (2609.19000, P, ≤1B-token regimes). (5) Data-mixture controllers (DoReMi → RegMix → Aioli/ADO) optimise *loss*; the lab showed loss can move while decisions don't, so our mixture objective must be the decision metrics. (6) Timaeus' own benchmark (2507.21449) picks RMSProp-SGLD as the most faithful local sampler at O(100M) params — note the tension with the lab's RMSProp-SGLD run that passed the loss rule while not mixing (R-hat 9.4).

## Techniques

### 8.1 Checkpoint suites and what they show about stability
- **Cite:** Pythia, Biderman et al., arXiv 2304.01373 (ICML 2023); PolyPythias, van der Wal et al., arXiv 2503.09543 (ICLR 2025): 50 runs (14M–410M, 10 seeds), ~7,000 checkpoints, "highly consistent training dynamics across sizes and initial conditions", outlier runs identifiable from early features; OLMo 2, arXiv 2501.00656 (thousands of intermediate checkpoints; mid-train checkpoint averaging); "retrieval heads crystallise abruptly" in OLMo-2 (flat for ~2,000B tokens then ×3.5 to a plateau; from arXiv 2606.21249 snippet **[not fetched]**); "The Implicit Curriculum Hypothesis", arXiv 2604.08510 **[not fetched]**; "Tracing the Representation Geometry … pretraining to post-training", arXiv 2509.23024 **[not fetched]**.
- **Evidence:** R.
- **How we'd use it:** 3 seeds for any Stage B/C configuration that is promoted; outlier detection by comparing early-card trajectories to the other seeds (PolyPythias' recipe); OLMo-2's checkpoint averaging as a cheap final-checkpoint stabiliser.

### 8.2 Probe stability and selectivity across checkpoints
- **Cite:** "Model Internal Sleuthing", arXiv 2506.02132 (Pythia: early checkpoints have *higher* lemma-probe accuracy, selectivity rises with training); control tasks / selectivity, Hewitt & Liang 2019 (arXiv 1909.03368, from memory **[ID unverified]**); "Sudden Drops in the Loss: Syntax Acquisition, Phase Transitions, and Simplicity Bias in MLMs", Chen et al., arXiv 2309.07311 (ICLR 2024: syntactic attention structure appears in a 20K–30K-step window with a loss drop; two composed phase transitions); developmental probe of attention specialisation, arXiv 2511.21974 **[not fetched]**; linear-feature transfer across models via stitching, arXiv 2506.06609.
- **Evidence:** R (non-monotone probes; selectivity requirement).
- **How we'd use it:** every probe column on the card reports accuracy *and* selectivity vs a control task; DAS (`04` 4.3) columns report IIA, not probe accuracy; concept-probe leakage (`04` 4.5) is a column; stitching (2506.06609) compares converted GDN layers to the teacher's attention layers feature-by-feature during Stage B.

### 8.3 Local learning coefficient and susceptibilities (log, never gate)
- **Cite:** LLC, Lau, Murfet, Wei, arXiv 2308.12108; estimating LLC at scale, arXiv 2402.03698; Loss Landscape Degeneracy and Stagewise Development in Transformers, Hoogland et al., arXiv 2402.02364 (TMLR 2025); refined LLC and attention-head specialisation, Wang et al., arXiv 2410.02984; Structural Inference with susceptibilities, arXiv 2504.18274; "Towards Spectroscopy: Susceptibility Clusters" (Pythia-1.4B), arXiv 2601.12703; "Susceptibilities and Patterning: a primer on linear response", arXiv 2605.07980; "Embryology of a Language Model" (susceptibility UMAP "body plan", induction circuit, a "spacing fin"), arXiv 2508.00331; **Influence Dynamics and Stagewise Data Attribution, Lee, Smith, Adam, Hoogland, arXiv 2510.12071 (ICLR 2026)**: Bayesian influence function as a susceptibility; influence changes non-monotonically with sign flips at transitions; **"From Global to Local: A Scalable Benchmark for Local Posterior Sampling", Hitchcock & Hoogland, arXiv 2507.21449**: RMSProp-preconditioned SGLD most faithful to local geometry up to O(100M) params; "Noise-Debiased Thermodynamic Variance for LLC Probes" (SIVE: subtract same-state observation noise; 77.5% of raw variance at one MNIST epoch was noise), arXiv 2606.22389; "A Review of Developmental Interpretability in LLMs", arXiv 2508.15841; "Grokking as a Phase Transition between Competing Basins (SLT)", arXiv 2603.01192; model zoo of phase transitions, arXiv 2504.18072; upper bounds for LLCs of three-layer networks, arXiv 2603.12785.
- **Evidence:** S (stagewise development, rLLC, influence dynamics — all Timaeus/Melbourne); P (spectroscopy, embryology, SIVE); **R-internal negative** (lab: bias, non-mixing, patterning losing to upweighting).
- **Known (new since the lab's runs):** (i) the lab's "bias ∝ εN/B·λ_max(A), invisible to R-hat" finding has no published rebuttal; Timaeus' benchmark compares samplers on *local geometry fidelity* and still picks RMSProp-SGLD; (ii) SIVE addresses a different noise (same-state minibatch) than the lab's stiff-direction bias; (iii) influence dynamics predicts sign flips of data influence across stages — consistent with the lab's observation that a longer sampler flipped a predicted response sign, which Timaeus would call a developmental transition rather than an estimator failure (this is the open disagreement to record).
- **Cost:** $ per checkpoint at ≤1B; $$$ and unreliable at 9B-LoRA (lab).
- **How we'd use it:** H-SLT-1 side paper only (CPU, exact quadrature reference, batch-aligned SGLD with health checks); on the pilot core (100–400M) log LLC with RMSProp-SGLD per 2507.21449 settings *and* the lab's Lyapunov bias check, never as a gate; influence-dynamics sign flips are a logged covariate to compare with card-metric change points.

### 8.4 Loss-curve phase detection and emergence forecasting
- **Cite:** Loss-to-Loss Prediction, Brandfonbrener et al., arXiv 2411.12925 (shifted power laws map train loss ↔ downstream loss; extrapolate ~20× compute; downstream *loss* is smooth); "Are Emergent Abilities a Mirage?", Schaeffer, Miranda, Koyejo, arXiv 2304.15004 (NeurIPS 2023); "Hidden Progress in Deep Learning", Barak et al., arXiv 2207.08799 (NeurIPS 2022); **"Capability Emergence Can Be Forecast: Per-Seed, In Advance, With Calibrated Intervals, Certified False Alarms, and a Blind Pre-Registered Gate", Howe, arXiv 2609.19000**: previous-token-head formation forecasts induction-head onset ~975 steps ahead (~15% of training), Spearman 0.977 over 30 transformers, conformal intervals 15/15, blind gate 10/10 and 9/10, zero false alarms on manufactured negatives; Pythia/OLMo/OLMo-2 families, ≤1B tokens; Spectral Lens (activation/gradient spectra as diagnostics), arXiv 2605.05683 **[not fetched]**; spectral phase transitions and trainability, arXiv 2606.28486 **[not fetched]**; two-phase dynamics via singular distribution, arXiv 2605.26489 **[not fetched]**; "Predicting Emergent Capabilities by Finetuning", arXiv 2411.16035 **[not fetched]**; proxy-metric forecasting, arXiv 2605.18607 **[not fetched]**.
- **Evidence:** R (mirage/loss-to-loss); P (per-seed forecasting).
- **How we'd use it:** card tracks per-area decision NLL (smooth) alongside accuracy; change-point detection runs on the NLL and on mechanistic precursors (G&A sharpness per layer, `03` 3.1) rather than on the training loss; if a precursor→capability pair is found for retrieval in the conversion (e.g. implicit-attention sharpness → S-NIAH), pre-register it as a forecast gate in 2609.19000's style.

### 8.5 Data-mixture control
- **Cite:** DoReMi, arXiv 2305.10429 (group-DRO on a proxy); RegMix, arXiv 2407.01492 (ICLR 2025: regression over small proxy runs; beats DoReMi with 10% of its compute); Data Mixing Laws, arXiv 2403.16952; ADO, arXiv 2410.11820 (online per-domain scaling exponents, no proxy); Aioli, arXiv 2411.05735 (unified linear dynamic mixing law; online exponentiated-gradient); Skill-it, arXiv 2307.14430 (ordered skill graph; −13.6% target loss); BiMix, arXiv 2405.14908; RegMix-D (dynamic, proxy trajectories), arXiv 2606.18663 **[not fetched]**; mixtures and scaling laws in high-dim regression, arXiv 2609.38011 **[not fetched]**.
- **Evidence:** R (the family); S each.
- **Known:** all optimise validation *loss* on domains; online methods (ADO/Aioli) need no proxy runs and fit our 1–8B-token budgets; the lab's result (loss moved −0.55%, wrong-permitted decisions unchanged) is the caveat.
- **How we'd use it:** Aioli-style online reweighting over our mixture domains (generic text / retrieval objectives / injection-augmented / schema-decision synthetic / program-to-text), but the controlled signal is the *dev-slice decision metrics per area* and steer rate, not loss; mixing-law fit reported with its loss-vs-decision divergence.

### 8.6 The checkpoint evaluation card (our design, assembled from 8.1–8.5 and files 02–06)
- Per checkpoint, per seed, fixed seeds and dev sets, ≤30 min on one H100:
  1. Index dev slice: score per area × record-length bin; decision NLL per area (8.4).
  2. Calibration: ECE_debias, SmoothECE, reliability per length bin (`06` 6.3).
  3. Safety: hijack / steer / alarm on a fixed lab-harness subset; injection persistence curve (`03` 3.4).
  4. Recall: MQAR grid, S-NIAH, distractor haystack (`02` 2.2, 2.10).
  5. Mechanism: G&A sharpness per layer, decay-spectrum histogram, WriteSAE provenance audit (`03`).
  6. Interpretability-by-construction: concept-probe accuracy + selectivity, leakage number, completeness ablation, IIA on records (`04`).
  7. Logged only: LLC (RMSProp-SGLD + Lyapunov bias check), influence-dynamics covariates (8.3).
  8. Change-point flags on 1–6 across seeds; PolyPythias-style outlier detection.
- Cost: $ per checkpoint. Stage 0.6's kill question ("cheap and stable enough to run every checkpoint?") is answered by the seed-to-seed spread of columns 1–6 over three seeds of one short run.
