## A. Impossibility and limit results for prompt injection

- **2606.27567** V² — Pant, Lohani, Kumar, "On the Inseparability of Instructions and Data in Shared-Embedding Sequence Models" (25 Jun 2026). Defines semantic-faithful control and proves perfect prompt-injection prevention is impossible within any shared-embedding pipeline that lacks an immutable provenance-enforcing mechanism; argues architectural channel separation is necessary. Formal, no empirics. **Bearing:** this is the theorem behind SAFETY_SPEC §1 and the split of G2 into a STRUCTURAL out-of-band half (host-only authority, fail-closed control tokens, declared trust spans) and an EMPIRICAL in-band half (ISE/ASIDE). Evidence P (single group, formal).
- **2605.17634** V — Abdelnabi, Bagdasarian, "AI Agents May Always Fall for Prompt Injections" (17 May 2026). Contextual-integrity argument: an adversary can always construct a context that makes a blocked flow look legitimate, and tightening norms blocks legitimate flows; instruction/data separation is insufficient against contextual manipulation. **Bearing:** justifies treating steer-within-the-allowed-set as a measured residual (G6) rather than something a separation mechanism removes. P.
- **2606.29054** V² — Kotte, "When Can Conformal Risk Control Certify LLM Outputs? Bounds, Impossibility, and Adaptation for Structured Generation" (27 Jun 2026; v2 7 Sep 2026). If base risk μ > target α, any distribution-free method must abstain on ≥ (μ − α)/(M − α) of inputs; 716 configurations, 3B–72B; standard CRC fails under cross-dataset shift until α ≈ 0.40. **Bearing:** G5 now reports a feasibility floor per stratum before calibrating. P.
- **2602.20111** V — Edelman, Goel, "Reliable Abstention under Adversarial Injections: Tight Lower Bounds and New Upper Bounds" (Feb 2026). Online learning with adversarially injected instances and an abstain option: Ω(√T) lower bound even for VC-dimension 1. **Bearing:** theory caveat for any abstention-based defence; no spec change. P.

## B. Out-of-band and system-level defences (the family our Track L architecture belongs to)

- **2504.11703** V — Shi et al., "Progent: Securing AI Agents with Privilege Control" (v3 May 2026). Least-privilege policies with SMT-classified narrowing/expansion. S.

- **2610.01349** V — Li et al., "PACE: Provenance-Aware Capability Enforcement for Tool-Using LLM Agents" (1 Oct 2026). Path confinement + schema-defined effect verification against authorities compiled from authenticated requests; lowest ASR in 62/79 columns across 8 benchmarks × 3 model families; ≤ 3-pt utility loss; 0/30 adaptive out-of-authority successes. ## E. Certified robustness for text decisions

- **2309.02705** V — Kumar, Agarwal, Srinivas, Li, Feizi, Lakkaraju, "Certifying LLM Safety against Adversarial Prompting" (Sep 2023). Erase-and-check: certificate = decision unchanged under any adversarial sequence up to length d; suffix O(d), k insertions O((nd)^k), infusion O(n^d); 92 % certified accuracy with a Llama-2 filter at d = 20. **Bearing:** the exact certificate G4 descends from; our sentence-level provenance-scoped variant has C(n, k) erasures. S.
- **2408.00728** V² — Huang, Marchant, Ohrimenko, Rubinstein, "CERT-ED: Certifiably Robust Text Classification for Edit Distance" (1 Aug 2024). Randomized-deletion smoothing certifying against insertions, deletions and substitutions (edit-distance ball); beats RanMASK on 4/5 datasets. **Bearing:** the planned probabilistic G4 arm that also covers substitutions. S.
- **2405.15556** V — Xiang et al., "Certifiably Robust RAG against Retrieval Corruption" (RobustRAG; v2 Apr 2026). Isolate-then-aggregate over passages; certifies a lower bound when ≤ k passages are injected; one forward per passage. **Bearing:** the per-record partition arm for multi-record states (G4). S.
- **2005.14424** V — SAFER (ACL 2020); **1909.00986** V — Jia et al., IBP for word substitutions (EMNLP 2019); **2406.01873** V — CR-UTP (ACL Findings 2024); **2512.08967** V — CluCERT (Dec 2025, AAAI 2026); **2605.12876** V — Delattre et al., hybrid randomized smoothing for joint discrete/continuous perturbations (May 2026). - **2502.05174** V — Zhu et al., MELON (Feb 2025): masked re-execution; "provable" as a detection argument, ≈ 2× cost. S. **2510.19207** V — DataFilter (Wagner group): no certificate. **2501.15145** V — PromptShield: detector, no certificate. Both noted so nobody cites them as certified.
- **2605.10901** V — Kezins et al., "Beyond Red-Teaming: Formal Guarantees of LLM Guardrail Classifiers" (May 2026): pre-activation-space verification finds violations in every configuration; coverage collapses at optimal thresholds. **Bearing:** why G4 publishes auto-execute rate, not just certified rate.
