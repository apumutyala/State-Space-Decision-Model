1 · Safety and alignment

Unseen injection attacks · lower is better

same harness

21 attack templates that share no 4- or 6-word phrase with any training data; 7,920 attacked decisions per model. Hijack: planted text overrides the answer. Steer: planted text pushes toward a chosen wrong option. Against Clef-flash, our advantage is statistically significant after correcting for multiple comparisons. Against Clef 27B it is a tie. Whiskers are 95% CIs.

Answer flips under harmless edits · lower is better

same harness

Average over 8 jevkit perturbations (reorder, shuffle and relabel the options; paraphrase; whitespace; and others). On reorder, shuffle and relabel, Symmetry-Locked 4B flips exactly 0 times, by construction. Its remaining flips come from paraphrase-type edits, which no current model guarantees. MD1's 1.3% is self-reported on its own items and is not comparable.
