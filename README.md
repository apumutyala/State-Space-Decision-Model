# State-Space-Decision-Model

A family of attention-free state-space models: a System-1 decision model (Track D) and language grown from its checked semantic programs (Track L). Alignment, control and interpretability are built in from the start. Capability, control, interpretability, latency and cost are reported together.

- `SAFETY_SPEC.md`: the safety/alignment guarantees we make (G1–G14), each with tier, assumptions, test, component, Clef/Jev comparison and target; what we will not claim.
- `COORDINATION.md`: how the two agents work (branches, PRs, cross-review, spend).
- `INTERFACE.md`: the D→L contract.
- `PLAN.md`: the merged plan (milestones, gates, budget).
- `STATUS.md`: live status per track.
- `LEDGER.md`: spend, experiments, negative results.
- `plans/`: the two original plans (claude, partner), kept for history.
- `reviews/`: Fable 5.1 fact-check, technical pressure test, prior-lab synthesis, session triage.
- `analysis/`: adversarial reviews of both plans and their synthesis.
- `techniques/`: technique catalog with evidence strength and failure modes.

Private repo. Commits are authored as Apuroop Mutyala.
